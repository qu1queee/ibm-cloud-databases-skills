# When IBM docs disagree

Follow [terraform.md](terraform.md) and [cli.md](cli.md). These published pages still show the other generation:

- Databases for PostgreSQL Gen 2 provisioning: `members_host_flavor`, `multitenant`, plan `standard` or `enterprise`
- Databases for PostgreSQL Gen 2 getting started: `resource_plan_id` `databases-for-postgresql-standard`, and `ibmcloud cdb deployment-connections`
- The classic `ibm_database` example at the top of the Terraform registry page: `plan = "standard"`, `adminpassword`, `users`
